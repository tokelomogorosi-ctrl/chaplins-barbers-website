Accessibility Statement

What we’ve tried to get right

We use semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, alt text on images, and sufficient contrast. The site works on mobile and screen readers.

What we know isn’t perfect yet

Small viewport text, the audio player UI, and some form labels may need improvement. We’re working on it.

What we promise

We’ll prioritize accessibility fixes and keep improving the site.
